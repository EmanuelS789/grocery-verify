Delete Your GroceryScouter Account

You can permanently delete your GroceryScouter account and its personal data at any time, directly in the app. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Delete from within the app

  1. Open GroceryScouter and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings (gear icon on the Home screen).
  3. Scroll to the Danger Zone and tap Delete Account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm. The deletion runs immediately and you are signed out.

What is permanently deleted

  • Your profile, including your email address and display name
  • Your store and loyalty program memberships
  • Your saved and favorite stores, and store color settings
  • Your shopping lists and their contents
  • Your price alerts and tracked items
  • Your price verifications and any moderation flags you filed
  • Your app usage records held on our servers

Scan images are not on this list because they are never stored. When you photograph a price tag, the image is transmitted only so the price can be read off it, and is discarded immediately afterward — there is nothing to delete.

What is kept, with your identity removed

Community content stays in the database so the shared price data everyone relies on keeps working. Your link to it is severed in one of two ways, depending on the kind of content.

  • Community price contributions (the prices you scanned and shared): the price records remain in the community database and the contributor field is emptied. They keep only item, price, store, and date — nothing that identifies you — and cannot be re-linked to you afterward.
  • Items, stores, and item size-links you created: these are transferred to an anonymous “deleted user” placeholder account. The content stays in the shared directory — including the map location of any store you added — but it is no longer connected to you, and the placeholder identifies no one.
  • Consent log: retained indefinitely as a compliance record of what you agreed to and when, with your account identifier removed.

Both routes are disclosed in our Privacy Policy (sections 4, 6, and 7). Before deleting, you can download a copy of your data in the app via Settings → Export My Data.

If you can’t access the app

Email us at groceryscoutersupport@gmail.com from the email address on your account and ask for your account to be deleted. We will verify that you control the account’s email address and then complete the same deletion described above.
